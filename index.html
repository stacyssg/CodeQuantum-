How to Play

  1. Pick a column at the top of the board.
  2. Your token drops to the lowest empty space in that column.
  3. Connect 4 in a row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.
  4. If the board fills up with no 4-in-a-row, the game ends in a draw.
